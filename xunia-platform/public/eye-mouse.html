Privacy + scope

This prototype uses the open-source Apache-2.0 MediaPipe Tasks Vision face-landmark model. Video frames are processed on-device in the browser; model assets are downloaded at startup. The browser may retain only your calibration coefficients in local storage. This public build controls XUNIA web targets; system-wide macOS cursor control requires the separate local accessibility companion because browsers cannot move the operating-system mouse pointer.
