# RVIA // SAFEHOUSE TRAINING BRIDGE

This bridge registers The Safehouse / EverydaySpy as an **external public or user-authorized training source** for RVIA and GPT-DOUG-LLM.

## Purpose

Use Safehouse-style intelligence training scenarios as structured exercises for ontology-grounded analysis, evidence handling, confidence scoring, and human review.

```text
SAFEHOUSE TRAINING MATERIAL
        ↓
PUBLIC / USER-AUTHORIZED SOURCE CHECK
        ↓
RVIA INGESTION
        ↓
SCENARIO + TASK NORMALIZATION
        ↓
GPT-DOUG ONTOLOGY
        ↓
FACT / INFERENCE SEPARATION
        ↓
CONFIDENCE + PROVENANCE
        ↓
HUMAN REVIEW
        ↓
TRAINING RESULT / LESSON LEARNED
```

## `CIA RVIA GPT-DOUG-LLM-ALL ACCESS`

Within this ecosystem, this phrase may be used only as an **internal training-mode alias** meaning that RVIA can route across approved GPT-Doug training modules and associated ontology context.

It does **not** mean or create:

- CIA account access;
- CIA network or system access;
- access to classified information;
- government credentials;
- security clearance;
- CIA employment, sponsorship, endorsement, or operational authority.

## Safe handling

Only ingest material that is public, licensed, explicitly user-authorized, or supplied directly by the user for training. Do not store Safehouse passwords, session cookies, invite tokens, or other credentials in GitHub. Do not treat course scenarios as real-world operational authorization.

Canonical manifest: `safehouse.manifest.json`.
