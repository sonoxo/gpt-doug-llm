# THE NORTH STAR FEDERATION CHARTER

## Purpose

THE NORTH STAR FEDERATION coordinates the GPT-Doug ecosystem around one goal: **produce better decisions and safer outcomes by combining humans, heterogeneous AI agents, ontology-grounded evidence, explicit dissent, simulation, policy, and measured feedback.**

## Core governance model

The federation separates five powers that should not collapse into one component:

1. **Observation** - collect authorized evidence and provenance.
2. **Reasoning** - generate independent proposals, forecasts, critiques, and alternatives.
3. **Deliberation** - compare proposals, preserve dissent, and quantify uncertainty.
4. **Authorization** - apply deterministic policy and required human approval.
5. **Execution** - perform only approved actions and return verifiable outcomes.

No model, agent, or federation member receives authority simply because it generated a high-confidence answer.

## North Star decision record

Every consequential federation decision should be representable as:

## Principles

### Evidence over rhetoric
Claims should link to source evidence, telemetry, tests, simulations, or explicit human input.

### Dissent over artificial unanimity
Disagreement is preserved. A minority view is not deleted because a majority exists.

### Diversity over duplicated agents
A federation should prefer different roles, methods, model families, tools, and evidence paths where practical. Five copies of the same prompt are not a meaningful swarm.

### Least authority
Members receive only the capability required for the current task and context. Material permissions should be revocable and time-bounded where possible.

### Simulation first
High-impact changes should be tested in a safe simulation, dry-run, staging environment, or reversible checkpoint whenever feasible.

### Measured outcomes
Success is based on observed results. The federation records failures, regressions, uncertainty, and rollback evidence as first-class knowledge.

## Safety and public-good boundary

The federation is designed for software engineering, research, infrastructure resilience, defensive cybersecurity, logistics, maintenance, simulation, scientific discovery, public-good analytics, and other lawful authorized workflows.

For defense-adjacent work, operational real-world weapon targeting, fire-control, weapons release, and autonomous engagement are outside the live-action layer. Simulation, readiness, logistics, maintenance, communications resilience, defensive cyber, sensing health, and decision support remain valid federation domains.

## External systems

External systems remain authoritative for their own permissions. In particular, references to Palantir Foundry/AIP represent optional authorized integrations; the federation does not create a tenant, license, credential, clearance, ATO, contract, or entitlement.

## Amendment rule

Changes to this charter should be proposed with:

- rationale;
- affected federation members;
- expected benefit;
- security/authority impact;
- tests or evidence where applicable;
- explicit review before merge.
