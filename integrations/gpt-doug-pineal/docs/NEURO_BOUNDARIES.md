# Non-invasive neurointerface research boundary

There are two different meanings of "brain":

**AI software brain (implemented):** A human-approved external memory/ontology store can be read and edited through a CLI or authenticated local API. LLM hidden weights, inaccessible private reasoning traces, and commercial provider infrastructure cannot be read or rewritten through these interfaces.

**Human biological brain (NOT implemented):** A non-invasive electroencephalogram can measure coarse electrical activity at the scalp. It does not generally reveal arbitrary thoughts or provide a method for uploading factual knowledge into a person. Non-invasive stimulation techniques exist under specialized research/clinical oversight but are NOT included here.

A future experimental acquisition adapter would require written and revocable consent, explicit recording indicators, local retention controls, biosignal privacy protections, a validated device SDK and electrode setup, research/ethics review where applicable, and independent evaluation of error rates and subject variability. Default must be read-only; no actuation or medical claims. The present code does not access human neurodata or stimulate anyone.
