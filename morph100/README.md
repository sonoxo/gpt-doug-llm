# MORPH 100 Global Problem-Solvers

100 distinct actionable problem definitions in 10 areas inspired by the UN 17 Sustainable Development Goals: https://sdgs.un.org/goals

## What was built
- Free dependency-free HTML/CSS/JS catalog of 100 defined problems, with search and category filters
- On-device pilot-plan generator customized for a region and audience
- Exportable structured JSON plans and clipboard sharing
- Free core resources with an optional human-contracted implementation service model
- Requested $Sonoxo payment preference displayed **without** access to payment systems

## What was not built
- 100 independently completed solutions, new government service integrations, domain-specific operational software, evidence of impact, customers or revenue
- Background agents, bank access, payout instructions on external platforms, or actual transfers

## Preview locally
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory morph100
```
Open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

## Boundaries
Plan outputs are templates requiring local verification, community engagement, and specialist review for health, legal, safety and financial contexts. No user entries are transmitted to a server. For paid deployments, sign a legitimate service agreement, verify the actual client and recipient and comply with relevant privacy, tax, and payment-provider rules. Static hosting may be free subject to provider terms. The public site is not deployed by this pull request.
