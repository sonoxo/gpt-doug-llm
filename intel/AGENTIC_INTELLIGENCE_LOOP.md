## Agent rules for intelligence

1. Preserve the original source reference and retrieval time.
2. Separate **source claim**, **verified fact**, **analytic inference**, and **unknown**.
3. Never turn missing captions, inaccessible media, or editorial framing into asserted fact.
4. Corroborate material claims before they become high-confidence ontology facts.
5. Give every analytic judgment a confidence label and basis.
6. Carry unresolved contradictions and intelligence gaps into the final brief.
7. Re-authorize every agent/model handoff and minimize transferred context.
8. Outside-model egress follows SHADOW GLASS data-governance policy.
9. Publication requires provenance, grounding evals, source links and audit receipt.
