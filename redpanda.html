Handling boundary

Public does not mean everything

Red Panda is designed to help move information deliberately between working, review, and publication planes. It does not infer that defense, intelligence, Palantir, government, or customer material is classified; it also does not automatically make sensitive material public.

🌎 PUBLIC_RELEASE

Sanitized material explicitly approved for publication, GitHub, demos, papers, and public web pages.

🏢 UNCLASSIFIED_INTERNAL

Ordinary private working material that should stay in controlled internal storage.

⚠️ RESTRICTED_REVIEW

NDA, customer, government-nonpublic, security-sensitive, CUI-candidate, export-review, or uncertain information. No automatic public promotion.

🔴 CLASSIFIED_MARKED

Officially classified material stays entirely outside ordinary GitHub, consumer Drive, Gmail, Lovable, and standard Red Panda publication paths.

Engineering contract

How to add capabilities safely

RuleMeaning
Adapter isolationNew services live behind adapters instead of embedding credentials in the core runtime.
Explicit scopesEvery adapter declares what it can read and what it can write.
Writes default offConsequential external mutation is disabled until intentionally configured.
Audit receiptsEvery agentic action should emit structured evidence of what happened.
Health gatesA capability is not GREEN until its relevant health check actually passes.
State separationAPPLIED, GREEN, DEPLOYED, CONNECTED, and AUTHORIZED mean different things and are never inferred from each other.
Product direction

From USB tool to agentic edge computer

The long-term Red Panda idea is not simply “a chatbot on a flash drive.” It is a portable edge control plane: local inference where possible, durable task state, defensive self-checks, governed file handling, explicit cloud adapters, and independently verifiable health. That lets one Red Panda node operate alone, then participate in a larger XUNIA fleet when authorized services are available.
