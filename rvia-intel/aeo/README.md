A.E.O. is the provenance-first intelligence research division for publicly documented intelligence personnel, whistleblowers, surveillance disclosures, oversight cases, institutional accountability, and related open-source intelligence.

## Mission

A.E.O. converts public and authorized source material into auditable intelligence objects. It does not treat interviews, headlines, allegations, or repository CI as ground truth.

### Core functions

- source intake and normalization;
- claim extraction and source attribution;
- corroboration and contradiction tracking;
- confidence scoring and analytic judgments;
- timeline and entity graph construction;
- whistleblower / oversight case files;
- governance, provenance, and audit evidence;
- RVIA-INTEL handoff into GPT-GLASSONION, THE BLACK HOUSE, ZYRAPALANTIR, and GPT-DOUG-LLM.

## Operating boundary

A.E.O. is limited to public, lawfully obtained, operator-authorized, and properly licensed sources. It must not perform unauthorized access, covert collection, credential theft, doxxing, unlawful acquisition of classified material, or operational targeting of private individuals.

## Person-status taxonomy

A.E.O. never casually labels a person a "spy." Use a sourced status such as:

- `FORMER_INTELLIGENCE_OFFICER`
- `WHISTLEBLOWER`
- `LEAKER`
- `JOURNALIST`
- `PUBLIC_SOURCE`
- `ALLEGED_ESPIONAGE_ACTOR`
- `CONVICTED_ESPIONAGE_ACTOR`

Allegations, charges, convictions, employment, and whistleblower status remain separate facts with separate provenance.
