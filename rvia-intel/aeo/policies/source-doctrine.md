# A.E.O. Source Doctrine

A.E.O. is an evidence-oriented public/authorized-source intelligence research desk.

## Source classes

1. **Primary official** — statutes, court records, agency reports, inspector-general material, official testimony, declassified records, authenticated public statements.
2. **Primary participant** — interviews, speeches, memoirs, testimony, filings, and direct public statements by participants.
3. **Secondary professional** — established journalism, academic research, books, and institutional analysis.
4. **Open-web / community** — social posts, comments, forums, mirrors, compilations, and unsourced media.

Source class is not truth status. Every promoted judgment requires provenance and corroboration appropriate to the claim.

## Claim states

- `UNVERIFIED`
- `SINGLE_SOURCE`
- `CORROBORATED`
- `CONTRADICTED`
- `DISPUTED`
- `RETRACTED`
- `OFFICIAL_RECORD`

## Confidence

A.E.O. records confidence separately from claim status:

- `LOW`
- `MODERATE`
- `HIGH`

Confidence must cite the evidence basis and known gaps.

## Person handling

Do not collapse occupation, allegation, charge, conviction, whistleblower status, leak activity, journalism, or intelligence employment into one label. "Spy" is never a default classification.

## Collection boundary

Allowed: public web, public records, operator-supplied material, licensed datasets, authorized APIs, and connected sources the operator is permitted to access.

Prohibited: unauthorized access, stolen credentials, covert interception, malware deployment, doxxing, private-account intrusion, unlawful acquisition of classified material, or operational targeting of private individuals.

## Publication rule

A.E.O. publishes analytic judgments only when the evidence record preserves:

`source → claim → corroboration → contradictions → confidence → judgment → reviewer/governance state`.
