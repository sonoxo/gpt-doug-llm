# Bidder evidence collection — owner action checklist

**DO NOT PUT PRIVATE FINANCIAL OR PERSONAL DOCUMENTS IN A PUBLIC GITHUB REPOSITORY.** Keep evidence in a restricted folder outside the repository; provide only a non-sensitive internal reference in the local gate overlay.

### Organizational and legal identity (all four)

- [ ] Exact legal entity and jurisdiction of incorporation
- [ ] Evidence of good standing/registered address (private)
- [ ] Authorized representative and signatory
- [ ] Mandatory tax identifiers and vendor registration proofs submitted only to authorized buyer channels
- [ ] Liability, professional indemnity / cybersecurity insurance policies if required
- [ ] Beneficial ownership, anti-corruption, sanctions and conflict-of-interest declarations using buyer forms
- [ ] Verify who owns and operates the intended receiving bank/payment account and that the buyer permits that method

### Capability and delivery (specific to tender)

- [ ] **ITC:** links to completed attributable audiovisual/digital media works; reference contacts with permission; Uganda language/location partner if mandatory; rights and consent workflows
- [ ] **UNDP Saudi:** actual translated/localized assets, graphic design and creative portfolio; named translators/reviewers; English tender package and functioning Quantum account
- [ ] **FAO Sri Lanka:** truly independent verification firm, grant monitoring/auditing references; conflict-of-interest controls; legitimate local verification team
- [ ] **SYMCA:** proven subscription platform, hosted payment processor, GPS integration, insurance partner, support staff, audited security and similar client engagements

### Original procurement attachments (critical)

- [ ] ITC RFQ cover letter
- [ ] ITC Annex A ToR
- [ ] ITC Annex B Technical Evaluation XLSX
- [ ] ITC Annex C Conditions
- [ ] UNDP current Quantum negotiation documents, schedules and any amendments
- [ ] FAO technical ToR, evaluation matrix, official price forms
- [ ] SYMCA primary tender, clarifications, submission channel and service-level requirements

### Final proposal review gate

- [ ] Every buyer requirement traced to a truthful document, demonstration, qualified person or signed declaration
- [ ] No invented testimonials, client contracts, certifications, financial statements, local office or staff
- [ ] Actual staffing and partner permissions confirmed in writing
- [ ] Detailed timeline, responsibility matrix, taxes and currency reconciled
- [ ] Bidder registration and legal/pricing documents reviewed privately
- [ ] Final version reconciled to latest addenda
- [ ] Signatory reviews and authorizes any external communication
- [ ] Submission only via the exact buyer-authorized portal/channel, never auto-submitted

## Ethical payment handling

Buyer contracts typically specify their own vendor-payments process and approved bank/KYC documents. The owner's Cash App handle $Sonoxo is a preference, **not a universally accepted institutional payout destination**. Never redirect UN or public procurement payments without explicit buyer approval and lawful ownership verification.
