## Proposed target architecture — not a claim of existing capability

**Customer portal:** Responsive, accessible enrollment, eligibility evidence upload, communications, self-service subscription controls and support requests.

**Subscription ledger:** Plans, invoices, payment state, refunds, suspensions, reconciliation and immutable transaction identifiers. Use an approved payments processor's hosted payment experience. Never store raw card data or route client subscription funds to $Sonoxo.

**Asset and telemetry layer:** Proven GPS/IoT device vendor/API, vehicle/lock status, location audit trail, asset reservations/allocations, consent and retention controls. No GPS partner or connected fleet has been verified.

**Service operations:** Ticket queue and escalation, service levels, theft/loss notices routed to a qualified insurer/claims handler, customer qualification checks and audit logging.

**Data governance:** UK GDPR/privacy notices, processor/controller roles, least-privilege access, retention policy, breach process, UK hosting if required by buyer, accessibility test reports and business continuity.

**Analytics:** Fleet utilization, subscription churn, unallocated bikes, technical alarms, response time and accessible exports with provenance.
