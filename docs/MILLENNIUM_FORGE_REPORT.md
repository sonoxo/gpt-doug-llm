## Seven specific mathematical deliverables

### 1. P versus NP — exact small SAT certificate

For three pigeons and two holes, the standard pigeonhole CNF has 6 Boolean variables and 12 clauses: at least one hole per pigeon, at most one hole per pigeon, and no two pigeons in one hole. Brute-force enumeration checks **all 64 assignments**, finding **zero** satisfying assignments. An *independent combinatorial count* gives zero injections from a three-element set into a two-element set. The 2x2 case yields **exactly two** satisfying assignments. Exhaustive tiny instances prove nothing about asymptotic P vs NP.

**Unsolved obligation:** Prove or disprove polynomial-time algorithms for NP-complete languages in general, rather than checking individual finite SAT instances.

### 2. Riemann hypothesis — rigorous rational enclosure for ζ(2)

Let `B>=3` be an integer, and define the **finite Euler product**

\[
P_B=\prod_{p\leq B}\frac{p^2}{p^2-1}.
\]

The Euler product for \(\zeta(2)\) converges. Dropping omitted factors gives a lower bound, and extending the prime-factor tail to *all integers* greater than B gives

\[
\boxed{P_B < \zeta(2) < P_B\frac{B+1}{B}}.
\]

The upper bound is **exactly justified** by telescoping \(\prod_{n=B+1}^{\infty}\frac{n^2}{n^2-1}=(B+1)/B\). At B=29, the executable obtains the exact enclosing interval and explicitly bounds its width. For each finite product and any complex \(s\) with \(\Re(s)>0\), \(1-p^{-s}\ne0\), but this **cannot be extended by limit** into the critical strip as if the infinite Euler product converged there.

**Unsolved obligation:** Prove every nontrivial zero of the analytically continued ζ lies on \(\Re(s)=1/2\).

### 3. Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer — an infinite **special** prime-family theorem

For the nonsingular elliptic curve \(E:y^2=x^3-x\), let \(a_p=p+1-\#E(\mathbb{F}_p)\), for odd prime p. Euler's criterion calculates every required quadratic character exactly. For every prime \(p\equiv3\ (\operatorname{mod}4)\), not merely the tested primes,

\[
\boxed{a_p=0}.
\]

**Proof:** \(f(x)=x^3-x\) is odd, so \(f(-x)=-f(x)\), and \(\chi_p(-1)=-1\) for such p. Therefore the character sum \(\sum_x\chi_p(f(x))\) cancels in pairs; the fixed point \(x=0\) contributes zero. Thus \(a_p=-\sum_x\chi_p(f(x))=0\). The program independently counts points for many primes and checks \(a_p^2\le4p\). This is classical complex-multiplication behavior, not a novel theorem.

**Unsolved obligation:** Relate the rank of rational points on *every* elliptic curve over Q to the order of vanishing of its L-function at s=1.

### 4. Hodge conjecture — exact algebraic generators for CP⁴

The known rational cohomology ring is \(H^*(\mathbb{CP}^{n},\mathbb Q)=\mathbb Q[h]/(h^{n+1})\), with \(\deg h=2\). Exactly one Hodge class exists in every bidegree \((k,k)\) with \(0\le k\le n\), generated by \(h^k\), the cycle class of a linear \(\mathbb{CP}^{n-k}\). For \(\mathbb{CP}^4\), the middle \((2,2)\) class has an algebraic representative \(\mathbb{CP}^2\), and \(\int_{\mathbb{CP}^4}h^4=1\).

**Unsolved obligation:** Establish the analogous algebraicity for rational Hodge classes on arbitrary smooth complex projective varieties, not just projective space.

### 5. Yang–Mills and mass gap — exactly solvable 2D Z₂ gauge model

A 2×2 planar square plaquette lattice with open boundary has **12 edges** and **4 plaquettes**. Enumerate all **2¹²=4096** Z₂ edge-sign assignments. Give each plaquette weight **4** if its product is +1 and **2** if it is −1. The rank of the plaquette-to-edge incidence map over GF(2) is **4**, so plaquette signs are independent. Exact partition function:

\[
Z=2^{12-4}(4+2)^4=331{,}776.
\]

For an axis-aligned rectangular Wilson loop enclosing area A plaquettes, interior edges cancel, giving

\[
\boxed{\langle W(C)\rangle=\left(\frac{4-2}{4+2}\right)^A=3^{-A}}.
\]

The program checks **all nine rectangular loops** of the 2×2 box. In particular, the outer Wilson loop expectation equals **1/81 exactly**. This is a classical discrete gauge-theory *toy* area law; it does not establish a positive mass gap for a continuum, four-dimensional, nonabelian quantum Yang–Mills theory.

**Unsolved obligation:** Construct and axiomatically control the 4D continuum quantum Yang–Mills theory and its strictly positive mass gap.

### 6. Navier–Stokes — exact smooth 3D unforced family

On the periodic torus \((\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z})^3\), with viscosity \(\nu=1/4\), consider

\[
\boxed{u(t,x,y,z)=(2e^{-t}\sin(2y),0,0),\quad p=0}.
\]

Direct calculation gives \(\nabla\cdot u=0\), \((u\cdot\nabla)u=0\), and \(\partial_tu-\nu\Delta u=0\) identically. The kinetic energy divided by volume is exactly \(e^{-2t}\); at t=0, \(\|\nabla u\|_2^2/\mathrm{Vol}=8\) and \(E'(0)/\mathrm{Vol}=-2=-\nu(8)\). This proves smoothness for this particular family only. The repository's earlier independent Beltrami/Fourier certificate engine provides a larger exact globally smooth subfamily.

**Unsolved obligation:** General unforced 3D global regularity. Forced blowup was claimed in a September 2026 proof; check Clay's review before asserting an officially settled prize.

### 7. Poincaré conjecture — independent exact S³ triangulation benchmark

The boundary of the 4-simplex is an explicit combinatorial triangulation of \(S^3\) with \((C_0,C_1,C_2,C_3)=(5,10,10,5)\). Rational Gaussian elimination on the oriented simplicial boundary matrices gives ranks \((4,6,4)\); \(\partial^2=0\) is checked entry-by-entry. Therefore

\[
\boxed{(b_0,b_1,b_2,b_3)=(1,0,0,1),\quad\chi=0}.
\]

Choosing the four edges \((0,i)\) as a spanning tree leaves six generators in the 2-skeleton's fundamental-group presentation; the six triangles \((0,i,j)\) kill every remaining generator. Hence the **fundamental group is trivial for this particular complex**. This checks a known example. **Perelman's geometrization/Ricci-flow proof** established the general Poincaré conjecture, which is already solved.
