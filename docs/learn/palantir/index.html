Palantir for Beginners

A visual, beginner-first guide to the ideas behind Palantir: connecting data, modeling the real world with Ontology, turning information into workflows in Foundry, using AIP with context and governance, and understanding how Gotham and Apollo fit into the larger platform.

The guide follows a non-human aura of computational energy searching for a place to node, process, and compute across CPU/GPU infrastructure—turning abstract platform concepts into a simple visual journey.

What you will learn

Six concepts. One connected mental model.

1 · What is Palantir?Connect data, reveal relationships, and support decisions.
2 · Core productsGotham, Foundry, AIP, and Apollo in plain language.
3 · OntologyObjects, properties, links, and actions as a shared model of reality.
4 · Foundry workflowIngest → clean → model → analyze → build apps → govern.
5 · AIP at workAsk, retrieve context, reason, propose action, and preserve human approval.
6 · Beginner roadmapLearn vocabulary, explore data, build one workflow, then practice a real use case.
