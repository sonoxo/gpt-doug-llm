# GPT-Doug CL1 Research Claim

**Status:** RESEARCH_ONLY  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-10-13

## Primary claim

GPT-Doug implements a provenance-aware computational research architecture that combines:

1. the official Cortical Labs CL1 software interface and SDK simulator for bounded neural-electrophysiology software experiments;
2. an evidence-grounded neuronal mitochondrial reference model;
3. a bounded public-research evidence swarm restricted to explicit HTTPS sources;
4. a Global Medical Research Ontology (GMRO) layer for normalized oncology evidence;
5. patient-derived organoid and molecular-response evidence as independent translational data domains; and
6. a deterministic hypothesis-ranking engine that exhaustively evaluates a bounded research space and prioritizes hypotheses by evidence proximity, falsifiability, translational relevance, and validation strength.

The system can therefore **generate, rank, compare, and provenance-link experimentally testable oncology and neurocomputing hypotheses** while preserving the distinction between simulated CL1 activity, biological neural-network evidence, tumor-organoid evidence, mitochondrial reference biology, and clinical-trial evidence.

## What is established by the implementation

The repository provides software capable of:

- running CL1 SDK simulator recordings without issuing physical stimulation commands;
- representing canonical neuronal mitochondrial anatomy with explicit source provenance;
- crawling only an allowlisted set of public research sources with bounded concurrency and response size;
- normalizing research evidence into structured machine-readable records;
- exhaustively evaluating a finite hypothesis space across oncology and neurocomputing dimensions;
- penalizing unsupported causal inferences, including inferring mitochondrial state directly from neural spike data;
- requiring independent replication, prospective holdout testing, orthogonal measurement, or registered clinical evidence before elevating a hypothesis;
- producing reproducible outputs suitable for human review and later experimental validation.

## Scientific claim boundary

The implementation **does not establish** any of the following:

- creation of a physical organoid;
- access to or control of a physical CL1 device;
- mitochondrial imaging of a specific CL1 neuron;
- direct ATP, ROS, membrane-potential, respiration, or mitochondrial-health measurement from CL1 telemetry;
- autonomous biological experimentation;
- a cancer cure;
- clinical efficacy of any treatment;
- authority to connect to arbitrary external, private, global, or critical infrastructure.

Any future claim involving physical CL1 neurons, organoids, metabolic state, therapeutic efficacy, or patient treatment requires direct measurement, appropriate biological provenance, independent replication, and applicable institutional, ethical, safety, and regulatory review.

## Strongest defensible research statement

> GPT-Doug is an evidence-fusion and hypothesis-search platform that can integrate CL1-compatible neural-computing software, organoid-response research, mitochondrial biology, and normalized oncology evidence to identify and prioritize falsifiable research hypotheses faster than manual evidence review alone, while retaining explicit provenance and validation gates.

This statement describes the software and its evidence workflow. It is not a claim of biological creation, therapeutic cure, or physical-device control.

## Validation target

A future stronger scientific claim would require a prospective study in which:

1. a hypothesis is generated before outcome data are revealed;
2. the relevant biological or clinical endpoint is independently measured;
3. the prediction is evaluated on a held-out dataset or separate experimental cohort;
4. the complete protocol, provenance, and analysis are reproducible; and
5. the result survives independent replication.

Only after those conditions are met should the corresponding claim be promoted from **HYPOTHESIS_ONLY** to **VALIDATED_RESEARCH_FINDING**.
