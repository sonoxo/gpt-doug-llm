Patent-inspired representation pipeline

Generalized from text/embedding/model/render concepts into a crypto research representation. XUNIA is not claiming that this browser page runs NeRFs or trains a hypernetwork.

01 · INGESTMarket data, protocol metadata, docs, governance, security and on-chain evidence.
02 · NORMALIZECanonical asset identity, rank, symbol, network, category, timestamp and provenance.
03 · VECTORIZEBuild a structured feature vector/embedding for comparison and downstream representation.
04 · REPRESENTCreate the asset hive state and deterministic visual identity from verified features.
05 · DOUG/CHAOSDoug synthesizes evidence; Chaos generates counterexamples and missing-data questions.
06 · VERIFYReconcile source disagreement, preserve uncertainty, version the result, then publish.

Asset hive contract

The market snapshot is only one layer. Deeper research workers can extend each namespace without overwriting provenance or uncertainty.

identity

Provider ID, symbol, name, network/category aliases.

market_snapshot

Rank, price, market cap, volume, supply, change windows and timestamp.

protocol

Consensus, architecture, issuance, utility and technical dependencies.

security

Audits, incidents, upgrade controls, bridges, custody and threat assumptions.

governance

Decision rights, admin authority, voting, treasury and concentration evidence.

ecosystem

Applications, integrations, developer signals and dependency graph.

doug_synthesis

Compact evidence-grounded state summary with confidence and unresolved gaps.

chaos_challenges

Counterexamples, source conflicts, missing evidence and assumptions to retest.

provenance

Source URLs, retrieval times, versions, transformations and verification status.
