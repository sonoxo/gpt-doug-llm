Canonical hive lifecycle

Every promoted lesson follows a governed path. No swarm directly overwrites the canonical ontology.

01 · SUMMONMint the logical swarm/hive job and assign a bounded domain.
02 · PROVENANCEIdentify builders, sources, prior state, and scope.
03 · EXPLOREDoug integrates known state while Chaos develops alternatives.
04 · BLACKBOARDWrite structured observations, hypotheses, artifacts, and confidence.
05 · CRITICChallenge claims, reproduce results, and locate contradictions.
06 · RECONCILEResolve duplicates, disagreement, dependencies, and conflicting evidence.
07 · VERIFYRequire observable evidence or retain the item as unverified.
08 · PROMOTEMove verified lessons into versioned canonical ontology state.
09 · REGRESSIONTest the updated state against known-good baselines and safety gates.
10 · BROADCASTMake approved state available to later authorized missions and nodes.

Hive namespace contract

Each HIVE-NNN namespace keeps reusable knowledge separate from the temporary swarm that produced it.

observations

What was directly observed, measured, retrieved, or produced.

hypotheses

Candidate explanations and proposed next tests.

sources

Source provenance, references, upstream ownership, and timestamps.

experiments

Reproducible tests, conditions, inputs, outputs, and postconditions.

failures

Rejected approaches, regressions, contradictions, and limits.

verified_lessons

Evidence-backed knowledge eligible for canonical promotion.

open_questions

Unresolved issues that should seed later swarms.

confidence

Explicit confidence rather than implied certainty.

provenance

Who/what contributed, where the artifact came from, and how it changed.
