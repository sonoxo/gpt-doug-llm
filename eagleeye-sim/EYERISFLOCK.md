# GPT-EYERISFLOCK

GPT-EYERISFLOCK is the AR situational-awareness layer for GPT-DOUG EAGLEEYE SIM.

## Mission
Fuse public, synthetic, and explicitly authorized sensor feeds into a spatial AR view with provenance, privacy, and human approval.

## Supported inputs
- Public weather and disaster feeds
- Public traffic and transit feeds
- Public aviation/maritime feeds where lawful and permitted
- User-owned cameras and sensors
- ROS2 / RoboParty simulated robot telemetry
- Lattice-shaped entity events
- Synthetic training feeds

## AR layers
- Horizon + compass ribbon
- Navigation waypoints
- Robot and vehicle state
- Hazard annotations
- Environmental overlays
- Sensor-health and network-health overlays
- Provenance and freshness badges
- Geofenced private zones
- Replay/history mode

## Privacy and safety
EYERISFLOCK does not provide:
- weapon sights
- fire-control or ballistic computation
- autonomous target selection
- strike planning
- person tracking across unrelated cameras
- face recognition
- covert surveillance of private spaces
- bypassing access controls

Any private sensor must be explicitly enrolled and auditable. Public feeds retain source/provenance metadata.
